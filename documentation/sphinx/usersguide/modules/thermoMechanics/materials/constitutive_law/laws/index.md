# <b>Constitutive Laws</b>

This section lists the constitutive laws available in OFFBEAT. Constitutive laws
define the stress–strain response, including elasticity, plasticity and
creep,

The constitutive law is selected in the material definition in `solverDict`
through the corresponding keyword(s) documented in each class page.

## <b>Classes</b>

Currently, OFFBEAT supports the following constitutive laws:

- [elasticity](../../../../classes/rheology/constitutiveLaws/elasticity.md)  
  linear elastic constitutive law.

- [hyperElasticity](../../../../classes/rheology/constitutiveLaws/hyperElasticity.md)  
  hyperelastic constitutive law.

- [neoHookeanElasticity](../../../../classes/rheology/constitutiveLaws/neoHookeanElasticity.md)  
  Neo-Hookean elastic constitutive law.

- [misesPlasticity](../../../../classes/rheology/constitutiveLaws/misesPlasticity.md)  
  von Mises plasticity constitutive law.

- [neoHookeanMisesPlasticity](../../../../classes/rheology/constitutiveLaws/neoHookeanMisesPlasticity.md)  
  Neo-Hookean elasticity with von Mises plasticity.

- [misesPlasticCreep](../../../../classes/rheology/constitutiveLaws/misesPlasticCreep.md)  
  coupled von Mises plasticity and creep constitutive law.

- [hyperElasticMisesPlasticCreep](../../../../classes/rheology/constitutiveLaws/hyperElasticMisesPlasticCreep.md)  
  hyperelasticity with coupled von Mises plasticity and creep.

- [fastNeutronGPLS](../../../../classes/rheology/constitutiveLaws/fastNeutronGPLS.md)  
  GPLS-based constitutive law with fast-neutron effects.

- [hydrogenGPLS](../../../../classes/rheology/constitutiveLaws/GPLS_Hydrogen.md)  
  GPLS-based constitutive law with hydrogen effects.
