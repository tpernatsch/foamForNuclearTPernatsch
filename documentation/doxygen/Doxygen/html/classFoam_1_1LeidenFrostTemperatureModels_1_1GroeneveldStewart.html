Detailed Description

Calculate the Leidenfrost Temperature at which the heat flux is minimal in the hysteresis after the CHF. The formula used is the same as TRACE with the correlation of GroeneveldStewart. WARNING, it is not complete yet, it lack the term due to quality.

Author: Gauthier Lazare (gauth.nosp@m.ier..nosp@m.lazar.nosp@m.e@gm.nosp@m.ail.c.nosp@m.om)
