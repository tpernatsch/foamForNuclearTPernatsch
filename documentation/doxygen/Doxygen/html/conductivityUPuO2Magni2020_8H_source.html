25  Foam::conductivityUPuO2Magni2020
26 
27 Description
28  Model for thermal conductivity of high-Pu content MOX. The used correlation
29  is taken from "Modelling and assessment of thermal conductivity and melting
30  behaviour of MOX fuel for fast reactor applications - A.Magni et al.".
31  (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnucmat.2020.152410)
32  The ranges of applicability of the correlation are the following:
33  - Temperature : [500,2700] K;
34  - Deviation from stoichiometry : [0,04];
35  - Plutonium content : [0,45] at.%;
36  - Porosity p: [0,7] %;
37  - Burnup : [0,130] GWd/tHM.
