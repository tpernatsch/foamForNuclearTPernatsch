◆ T_

scalarField T_
mutableprotected

Temperature field imposed at the boundary.

NOTE: the distinction between "oxideTemperature" and "value" becomes necessary when an oxide layer is present. In this case, "value" is the temperature of the oxide/material interface, while "oxideTemperature" is the outer oxide temperature. If the oxide is absent, the two are the same, i.e. the outer material temperature. This might change if we include the oxide layer in the model explicitly.
