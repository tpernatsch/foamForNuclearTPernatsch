Detailed Description

Rheme correlation for pressure drop.

\[ P_t = D_p + 1.0444 D_w \\ P_{w,pin} = N_p \pi (D_p + D_w) \\ B = \sqrt{\frac{P_t}{D_p}} + \left( 7.6 \frac{D_p+D_w}{L_w} \left( \frac{P_t}{D_p} \right)^2 \right)^{2.16} \\ \\ A = \frac{P_{w,pin}}{P_{w,pin} + P_{w,wrap}} \\ B_1 = 64 \sqrt{B} \\ B_2 = 0.0816 B^{0.9335} \\ \\ f_d = A \left( \frac{B_1}{Re} + \frac{B_2}{Re^{0.133}} \right) \]

where

\( N_p \) = Number of pins
\( D_p \) = Pin diameter
\( D_w \) = Wire diameter
\( L_w \) = Wire lead length (i.e pitch of the wrapper wire)
\( P_{w,pin} \) = Wetted pin perimeter
\( P_{w,wrap} \) = Wetted wrapper perimeter 
Example of mandatory dictionary entries:
\verbatim
numberOfPins        37;
pinDiameter         0.006000;
wireDiameter        0.0016;
wireLeadLen         0.1;        //- I.e. pitch of the wrapper wire
wetWrapPerimeter    0.175237;   //- Wetted wrapper perimeter (wrapper only)

Reference publications: Klaus Rehme, "PRESSURE DROP CORRELATIONS FOR FUEL ELEMENT SPACERS", Nuclear Technology, 1973, Vol. 17, 1, pages 15-23, DOI:10.13182/NT73-A31250.

Author: Stefan Radman (sradm.nosp@m.an@p.nosp@m.roton.nosp@m.mail.nosp@m..com / stefa.nosp@m.n.ra.nosp@m.dman@.nosp@m.epfl.nosp@m..ch)
