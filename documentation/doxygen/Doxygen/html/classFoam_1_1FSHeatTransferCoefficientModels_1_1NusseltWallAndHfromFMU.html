Detailed Description

Heat transfer coefficient for forced convective flows that is computed starting from the Nusselt, which is turn obtained via correlation in the form.

Nu = A_+B_*(Re^C_)*(Pr^D_)

with Re, Pr being the Reynolds (superficial in two-phase) and Prandlt of the fluid, and A, B, C, D selectable consts

Additional heat transfer coeff to take into account the wall resistance, such that

1/H = (1/(Nu * kappa / Dh)) + (1/H_wall) + (1/ H_fromFMU)

Example of mandatory dictionary entries: 

    const   4;
    coeff   0.01;
    expRe   0.8;
    expPr   0.8;
    addH    10000;
    HNameFromFMU   HCoupled; //This is the name given to the H in OpenFOAM
