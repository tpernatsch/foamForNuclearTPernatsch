44 Description
45  Mixture k-epsilon turbulence model for two-phase gas-liquid systems
46 
47  The basic structure of the model is based on:
48  \verbatim
49  Behzadi, A., Issa, R. I., & Rusche, H. (2004).
50  Modelling of dispersed bubble and droplet flow at high phase fractions.
51  Chemical Engineering Science, 59(4), 759-770.
52  \endverbatim
53 
54  but an effective density for the gas is used in the averaging and an
55  alternative model for bubble-generated turbulence from:
56  \verbatim
57  Lahey Jr, R. T. (2005).
58  The simulation of multidimensional multiphase flows.
59  Nuclear Engineering and Design, 235(10), 1043-1060.
60  \endverbatim
61 
62  The default model coefficients are
63  \verbatim
64  mixtureKEpsilonCoeffs
65  {
66  Cmu 0.09;
67  C1 1.44;
68  C2 1.92;
69  C3 C2;
70  sigmak 1.0;
71  sigmaEps 1.3;
72  }
73  \endverbatim
74 
75  Last modified by Stefan Radman for compatibility with FFSEulerFoam
76  (stefan.radman@epfl.ch, stefanradman92@gmail.com)
