25  Foam::Zy_CSED_RIA_EPRI
26 
27 Description
28  Clad failure criterion during the PCMI phase of a RIA transient based on the strain energy density (SED).
29  The complete description of this model is available at : https://www.epri.com/research/products/1021036
30  If the average SED on an axial slice goes over a critical value, the clad is considered to have failed.
31  The SED field corresponds to the strain energy density.
32  The CFP field corresponds to the SED field over the critical value. Thus if CFP > 1, the clad is considered failed.
33
