Detailed Description

Subclass for SN neutronics (Discrete Ordinate) [@FIORINA201524] [@Fiorina2015ApplicationCodes].

$$ \begin{align} \frac{1}{v_i} \frac{d \Omega_j \cdot \phi_{i,j}}{dt}

  • \nabla \cdot (\Omega_j \cdot \phi_{i,j})
  • \Sigma_{t,i} \phi_{i,j} & = S_{i,j}^I + S_{i,j}^A + S_i^D \quad \quad \forall i \wedge j \in [1; G] \times [1; M] \ \frac{\partial C_k}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot (\mathbf{u} C_k) & = \frac{\beta_k}{k_{eff}} \sum_{i'=1}^G \nu \Sigma_{f,i'} \phi_{i'}
  • \lambda_k C_k \quad \forall k \in [1; N] \end{align} $$

with the external source:

$$ \begin{align} S_{i,j}^I & = \sum_{i'=1}^G \sum_{j'=1}^M \omega_{j'} \phi_{i',j'} \left( \frac{1}{k_{eff}} \nu \Sigma_{f,i'} \chi_{p,i} + \Sigma_{s,i' \rightarrow i} \right) \ S_{i,j}^A & = \sum_{i'=1}^G \sum_{j'=1}^M \omega_{j'} \phi_{i',j'} \Sigma_{s,i' \rightarrow i} \left( \sum_{l=1}^p s_{i' \rightarrow i}^l \frac{2l+1}{2} P_l (\Omega_j \cdot \Omega_{j'}) \right) \ S_i^D & = \chi_{d,i} \sum_k \lambda_k C_k \end{align} $$

The anisotropic scattering terms are considered adopting a pth order Legendre polynomial expansion of the scattering cosine \( (\Omega_j \cdot \Omega_{j'}) \)

\( \phi_{i,j} \) = Neutron flux if the energy group i and direction j
\( C_k \) = Precursor k
\( S_{i,j}^I \) = Isotropic source term
\( S_{i,j}^A \) = Anisotropic source term
\( S_i^D \) = Delayed neutron source term
\( \Sigma_{t,i} \) = Total cross-section of the energy group i
\( \nu \Sigma_{f,i'} \) = Fission neutron production cross-sections of the energy group i'
\( \Sigma_{s,i' \rightarrow i} \) = Scattering cross-section from energy i' to energy i
\( k_{eff} \) = Multiplication factor
\( \chi_{p,i} \) = Prompt emission neutrons emitted in the energy group i
\( \chi_{d,i} \) = Delayed emission neutrons emitted in the energy group i
\( \lambda_k \) = Decay constant of the precursor group k
\( \beta_k \) = Delayed neutron fraction for the kth delayed neutron precursor group
\( \omega_{j'} \) = Normalized quadrature weight associated to the direction \( \Omega_{j'} \)
\( s_{i \rightarrow i'}^l \) = lth Legendre coefficient of the scattering cross-section \( \Sigma_{s,i \rightarrow i'} \)
\( P_l \) = lth Legendre polynomial
\( \mathbf{u} \) = Fuel velocity
\( v_i \) = Average neutron velocity of the energy group i
\( \Omega_j \) = Unit vector of the angular direction j
