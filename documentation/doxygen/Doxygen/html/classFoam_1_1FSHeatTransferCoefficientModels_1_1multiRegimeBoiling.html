Detailed Description

Heat transfer coefficient that covers many regimes, from single phase convection, two-phase convection, sub-cooled and nucleate boiling, to film boiling (the latter, hopefully).

Author: Stefan Radman (sradm.nosp@m.an@p.nosp@m.m.me / stefa.nosp@m.n.ra.nosp@m.dman@.nosp@m.epfl.nosp@m..ch)
