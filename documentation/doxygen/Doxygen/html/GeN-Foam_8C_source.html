41 Description
42  Multi-physics solver for nuclear reactor analysis. It couples a multi-scale
43  fine/coarse mesh 3-phase (liquid, vapour, porous substructure) sub-solver
44  for thermal-hydraulics, various sub-solvers for neutronics,
45  a displacement-based sub-solver for thermal-mechanics. The
46  thermal-hydraulic sub-solver consists of the custom developed FFSEulerFoam
47  solver (https://gitlab.com/virmodoetiae/FFSEulerFoam). It is capable of
48  modelling single and two-phase flows, while modelled fuel types consist
49  of either liquid fuel (e.g. MSRs) or fuel pin lattices. For the latter,
50  the energy dynamics is represented via a 1.5-D finite difference model.
