class  RezkallahSims
 Two-phase flow enhancement factor for the convective heat transfer coefficient based on the work of K.S. Rezkallah and G.E. Sims, "An Examination of Correlations of Mean Heat Transfer Coefficients in Two-Phase Two-Component Flow in Vertical Tubes," AIChE Symposium Series, 83 (257), 109-114, 1987. The TRACE code typically uses this over the Chen model due to greater numerical stability of this approach, particularly for low-pressure, low mass flow scenarios (see https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML1200/ML120060218.pdf, pp. 263-267) More...
