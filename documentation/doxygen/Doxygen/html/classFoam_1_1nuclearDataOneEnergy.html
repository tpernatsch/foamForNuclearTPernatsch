Detailed Description

Subclass for nuclear data handling. The interpolation scheme used is based on the polyharmonic spline radial basis function (RBF). The RBF used is linear. This class is able to interpolate any number of perturbed states for any number of parameters.

\[ \sigma_{E_g, \Omega_l}(\textbf{x}) = \sum_{i=1}^{N_{perturb}} w_i \phi(\|\textbf{x} - \textbf{c}_i\|) + v_0 + \sum_{k=1}^{N_{param}} v_k x_k \]

\( \textbf{x} \) = Parameter vector of size \( N_{param} \): \( \{ T_{fuel}, T_{clad}, T_{cool}, T_{struct}, \rho_{cool}, \alpha_{axial}, \alpha_{radial} \} \)
\( \textbf{c}_i \) = \( N_{perturb} \) data points to interpolate, each data point is of size \( N_{param} \)
\( \textbf{w} \) = Weights of the RBF of size \( N_{perturb} \)
\( \textbf{v} \) = Polynomial weights of size \( N_{param} \)

The basis function form used is linear

\[ \phi(\| \textbf{x} - \textbf{c}_i \|) = \| \textbf{x} - \textbf{c}_i \| \quad (\phi(r) = | r |) \]

The weights \( w_i \) and \( v_j \) are determined such that the function interpolates \( N_{perturb} \) given points \( (\textbf{c}_i, \sigma_{i}) \) (for i = 1, 2, …, \( N_{perturb} \)) and fulfills the \( N_{param} + 1 \) orthogonality conditions

\[ \sum_{i=1}^{N} w_{i} = 0 \quad \quad \sum_{i=1}^{N} w_{i} \textbf{c}_{i} = \textbf{0} \]

All together, these constraints are equivalent to the symmetric linear system of equations

\[ \begin{bmatrix} A & B \\ B^{\textrm {T}} & 0 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \textbf{w} \\ \textbf{v} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \sigma \\ \textbf{0} \end{bmatrix} \]

where

\[ A_{i,j} = \phi(|\textbf{c}_i - \textbf{c}_j|) \quad \quad B = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 \\ \textbf{c}_1 & \textbf{c}_2 & \cdots & \textbf{c}_N \end{bmatrix}^{\textrm {T}} \quad \quad \sigma = \left[ \sigma_1, \sigma_2, \ldots, \sigma_N \right]^{\textrm {T}} \]
