24 Class
25  Foam::sliceMapper
26 
27 Description
28  Parent class for slice mapper. This class creates a mapping framework for
29  passing information from an arbitrary OpenFOAM fuel geometry to an idealized
30  1D pin geometry (or viceversa).
31  Essentially the class superimposes a virtual, structured, axial 1D grid
32  on the actual mesh and uses the resulting slices for various operations.
33 
34  Used because:
35  - many quantities are calculated on the basis of 1.5D slice average
36  fields (e.g. the relocation needs the slice average burnup or power)
37  - when performing 1.5D simulations, it is necessary to perform a slice
38  per slice balance of axial forces to correctly take into account the
39  plenum spring and axial friction forces.
40  - averaging and min/max operations over slices might be useful for
41  developing output and or plotting toold, mainly for base irradiation
42  - might be useful for comparison against standard tools
43  - behavioral models that are based on 1-D assumptions and that in the
44  lack of better solutions are still needed in 3-D simulations (e.g.
45  classical relocation models)
46 
47  The different daugther classes might follow different schemes to split the
48  geometry (e.g. cellSets, by material, etc.). However the basic mechanism
49  remains the same.
50 
51  The basic idea is that the slice mapper will provide a mapping between the
52  actual 3-D/2-D geometry of OFFBEAT and a division in axial slices,
53  superimposed to the geometry. That is, the mapper splits the geometry in
54  axial slices and for each slice registers the IDs of the cells that belong
55  to that slice.
56 
57  The parent class can be selected with the "none" typename.
58 
59 Usage
60  In solverDict file:
61  \verbatim
62  sliceMapper none;
63  \endverbatim
