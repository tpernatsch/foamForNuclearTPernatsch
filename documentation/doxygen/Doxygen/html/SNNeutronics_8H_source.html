41 Description
42  Subclass for SN neutronics (Discrete Ordinate) [@FIORINA201524] [@Fiorina2015ApplicationCodes].
43 
44 $$
45 \begin{align}
46  \frac{1}{v_i} \frac{d \Omega_j \cdot \phi_{i,j}}{dt}
47  + \nabla \cdot (\Omega_j \cdot \phi_{i,j})
48  + \Sigma_{t,i} \phi_{i,j}
49  & = S_{i,j}^I + S_{i,j}^A + S_i^D
50  \quad \quad \forall i \wedge j \in [1; G] \times [1; M] \\
51  \frac{\partial C_k}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot (\mathbf{u} C_k) & =
52  \frac{\beta_k}{k_{eff}} \sum_{i'=1}^G \nu \Sigma_{f,i'} \phi_{i'}
53  - \lambda_k C_k
54  \quad \forall k \in [1; N]
55 \end{align}
56 $$
57 
58 with the external source:
59 
60 $$
61 \begin{align}
62  S_{i,j}^I & = \sum_{i'=1}^G \sum_{j'=1}^M \omega_{j'} \phi_{i',j'} \left(
63  \frac{1}{k_{eff}} \nu \Sigma_{f,i'} \chi_{p,i} + \Sigma_{s,i' \rightarrow i}
64  \right) \\
65  S_{i,j}^A & = \sum_{i'=1}^G \sum_{j'=1}^M \omega_{j'} \phi_{i',j'} \Sigma_{s,i' \rightarrow i} \left(
66  \sum_{l=1}^p s_{i' \rightarrow i}^l \frac{2l+1}{2} P_l (\Omega_j \cdot \Omega_{j'})
67  \right) \\
68  S_i^D & = \chi_{d,i} \sum_k \lambda_k C_k
69 \end{align}
70 $$
71 
72 The anisotropic scattering terms are considered adopting a pth order Legendre
73 polynomial expansion of the scattering cosine \f$ (\Omega_j \cdot \Omega_{j'}) \f$
74 
75 \vartable
76  \phi_{i,j} | Neutron flux if the energy group i and direction j
77  C_k | Precursor k
78  S_{i,j}^I | Isotropic source term
79  S_{i,j}^A | Anisotropic source term
80  S_i^D | Delayed neutron source term
81  \Sigma_{t,i} | Total cross-section of the energy group i
82  \nu \Sigma_{f,i'} | Fission neutron production cross-sections of the energy group i'
83  \Sigma_{s,i' \rightarrow i} | Scattering cross-section from energy i' to energy i
84  k_{eff} | Multiplication factor
85  \chi_{p,i} | Prompt emission neutrons emitted in the energy group i
86  \chi_{d,i} | Delayed emission neutrons emitted in the energy group i
87  \lambda_k | Decay constant of the precursor group k
88  \beta_k | Delayed neutron fraction for the kth delayed neutron precursor group
89  \omega_{j'} | Normalized quadrature weight associated to the direction \f$ \Omega_{j'} \f$
90  s_{i \rightarrow i'}^l | lth Legendre coefficient of the scattering cross-section \f$ \Sigma_{s,i \rightarrow i'} \f$
91  P_l | lth Legendre polynomial
92  \mathbf{u} | Fuel velocity
93  v_i | Average neutron velocity of the energy group i
94  \Omega_j | Unit vector of the angular direction j
95 \endvartable
