25  Foam::mazars2015_RIA
26 
27 Description
28  Damage model for UO2.
29  This model describes the loss of stiffness of the material by reducing the elastic modulus
30  as the damage increases: E = (1-D)*E.
31 
32  The model itself is the so called u-model, based on the work of J. Mazars et al.
33 
34  The present model is a parametrization of this model proposed by M. Reymond et al
35  to simulate laser heating experiments conducted on UO2. The thermo-mechanical state
36  of the ceramic reached during these experiments are close to the ones encountered during a RIA.
37 
38  This model is implemented in OFFBEAT for RIA simulations in order to simulate fuel cracking
39  and fragmentation during the transient.
40  The calculated damage value can also be used to drive the transient fission gas release.
41 
42  We thank F. Hamon (EdF R&D) and T. Helfer (CEA) for the numerical implementation available
43  in the MFront software from which the OFFBEAT implementation is largely based on.
