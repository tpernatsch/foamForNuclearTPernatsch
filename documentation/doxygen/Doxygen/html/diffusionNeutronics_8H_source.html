41 Description
42  Subclass for diffusion neutronics [@FIORINA201524] [@Fiorina2015ApplicationCodes] [@FIORINA2016212].
43 
44 The module solves the multi-group diffusion equations:
45 
46 $$
47 \begin{align}
48  \frac{1}{v_g} \frac{\partial \phi_g}{\partial t} = &
49  \nabla D_g \nabla \phi_g
50  + \frac{\chi_{p,g}}{k_{eff}} (1-\beta_{tot}) \nu \Sigma_{f,g} \phi_g \\
51  & - \Sigma_{r,g} \phi_g
52  + \frac{\chi_{p,g}}{k_{eff}} (1-\beta_{tot}) S_{n,g} \\
53  & + \chi_{d,g} S_d
54  + S_{s,g}
55  + S_g
56  \quad \forall g \in [1; G]
57 \end{align}
58 $$
59 
60 $$
61  \frac{\partial C_k}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot (\mathbf{u} C_k) =
62  \frac{\beta_k}{k_{eff}} \sum_h \nu \Sigma_{f,h} \phi_h
63  - \lambda_k C_k
64  \quad \forall k \in [1; N]
65 $$
66 
67 with the explicit source term:
68 
69 $$
70 \begin{align}
71  S_{n,g} & = \sum_{h \neq g} \nu \Sigma_{f,h} \phi_h \\
72  S_d & = \sum_k \lambda_k C_k \\
73  S_{s,g} & = \sum_{h \neq g} \Sigma_{h \rightarrow g} \phi_h
74 \end{align}
75 $$
76 
77 \vartable
78  \phi_g | Neutron flux for the gth energy group
79  C_k | Concentration of the kth delayed neutron precursor group
80  D_g | Neutron diffusion coefficient for the gth energy group
81  \chi_{p,g} | Prompt neutron yield for the gth energy group
82  \chi_{d,g} | Delayed neutron yield for the ith energy group
83  k_{eff} | Effective multiplication factor
84  \beta_{tot} | Total delayed neutron fraction
85  \beta_k | Delayed neutron fraction for the kth delayed neutron precursor group
86  \nu | Average number of neutrons per fission
87  \Sigma_{f,g} | Fission cross section for the gth energy group
88  \Sigma_{r,g} | Removal (disappearance) cross section for the gth energy group
89  \Sigma_{h \rightarrow g} | Group-transfer cross section from the hth to the gth energy group
90  S_{n,g} | Fission neutron source from neutron energy groups others than the gth
91  S_d | Delayed neutron source
92  S_{s,g} | Scattering neutron source from neutron energy groups others than the gth
93  S_g | External neutron source for the gth energy group
94  \mathbf{u} | Fuel velocity
95  v_g | Average neutron velocity for the gth energy group
96  \lambda_k | Decay constant for the kth delayed neutron precursor group
97 \endvartable

100 Usage
101 
102  In the neutronicsProperties file, for keff calculation in a critical
103  reactor:
104  \verbatim
105  model diffusionNeutronics;
106  eigenvalueNeutronics true;
107  \endverbatim
108 
109  For an subcritical reactor driven by an external neutron source:
110  \verbatim
111  model diffusionNeutronics;
112  eigenvalueNeutronics false;
113  externalSourceNeutronics true;
114  \endverbatim
