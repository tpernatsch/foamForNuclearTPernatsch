Detailed Description

Model for the prediction of the Leidenfrost temperature (i.e. T_min) for which the heat flux is minimal. After this temperature, we are in post CHF Regime.

Author: Gauthier Lazare (gauth.nosp@m.ier..nosp@m.lazar.nosp@m.e@gm.nosp@m.ail.c.nosp@m.om)
