38 Class
39  Foam::FSDragCoefficientModels::ReynoldsPower
40 
41 Description
42  Drag coefficient in the form
43  \f[
44  f_d = A + B \times Re^C \\
45  K_d = 0.5 f_d \frac{\alpha \rho |U|}{D_h}
46  \f]
47 
48  where:
49  \vartable
50  Re | Reynolds number (superficial in two-phase) of the fluid
51  A, B, C | Selectable constants
52  \endtable
53 
54  Example of mandatory dictionary entries (with this coefficients, this
55  is the Blasius correlation):
56  \verbatim
57  coeff 0.316;
58  exp -0.25;
59  \endverbatim
