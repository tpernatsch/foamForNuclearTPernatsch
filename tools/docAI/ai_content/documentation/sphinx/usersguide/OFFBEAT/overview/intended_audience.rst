Intended audience
=================

OFFBEAT is primarily intended for researchers, engineers, and graduate students involved in
the modelling and analysis of nuclear fuel behaviour.

The code is designed for users who:

- are interested in continuum-scale modelling of fuel and cladding behaviour,
- require access to multi-dimensional and non-axisymmetric analyses,
- are comfortable working with open-source simulation tools,
- seek transparency and extensibility in physical modelling and numerical methods.

.. warning::

   OFFBEAT builds on the OpenFOAM framework and follows its general concepts and workflows.
   Users are therefore expected to be familiar with the basic principles of OpenFOAM, or willing
   to acquire this familiarity when working with OFFBEAT.

While OFFBEAT can be applied to engineering-scale problems, it is not intended as a black-box
industrial licensing tool. Users are expected to engage with the modelling assumptions,
numerical settings, and physical models adopted in a given analysis.
