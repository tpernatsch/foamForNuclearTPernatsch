\subsection{Detailed Description}
Heat transfer coefficient for forced convective flows that is computed starting from the \hyperlink{classFoam_1_1FFHeatTransferCoefficientModels_1_1Nusselt}{Nusselt}, which is turn obtained via correlation in the form. 

\label{_Description}%
 Nu = A\+\_\++\+B\+\_\+$\ast$(Re$^\wedge$\+C\+\_\+)$\ast$(Pr$^\wedge$\+D\+\_\+)

with Re, Pr being the Reynolds (superficial in two-\/phase) and Prandlt of the fluid, and A, B, C, D selectable consts

Author Stefan Radman (\href{mailto:sradman@pm.me}{\tt sradman@pm.\+me} / \href{mailto:stefan.radman@epfl.ch}{\tt stefan.\+radman@epfl.\+ch})

Dictionary entries example (mandatory) const 4; coeff 0.\+01; exp\+Re 0.\+8; exp\+Pr 0.\+8;
