\subsection{Detailed Description}
Model for representing a nuclear fuel pin with constant material properties. Models fuel, gap, cladding and solves for the heat equation with a finite differences scheme. Only radial heat diffusion is considered. Derivation of the equations is provided at the end of the present file.
