\subsection{Detailed Description}
This class describes the latent heat of vaporization of water in the 0.\+01-\/350 C range. It was obtained from a fit (in the form of L = A+\+B$\ast$log(C-\/T) with T being the liquid temperature (K)) of N\+I\+ST water data at saturation found at\+: \href{https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/srd/NISTIR5078-Tab1.pdf}{\tt https\+://www.\+nist.\+gov/system/files/documents/srd/\+N\+I\+S\+T\+I\+R5078-\/\+Tab1.\+pdf}. 

\label{_Description}%
 It performs reasonably well up to $\sim$ 365 C as well, considering that the critical water temperature is 375.\+16 C (at which point the latent heat becomes 0, but is limited for obvious numerical reasons as this code is not meant for sub-\/supercritical fluid transition simulations)
