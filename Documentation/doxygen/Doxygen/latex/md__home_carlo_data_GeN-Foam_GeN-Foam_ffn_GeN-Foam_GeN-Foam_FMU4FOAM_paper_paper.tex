\section*{Summary}

Accurate C\+FD simulations often require complex boundary conditions that may be depend on numerous other engineering disciplines. Currently, these boundary conditions would require the addition of new models to the open-\/source C\+FD framework Open\+F\+O\+AM which is error-\/prone and time-\/consuming. F\+MI standard offers a container (F\+M\+Us) to exchange multi-\/physics models that can be used to simulate these multi-\/physics. This library provides the possibility of coupled F\+M\+Us with Open\+F\+O\+AM or exporting Open\+F\+O\+AM as an F\+MU.

\section*{Statement of need}

Modeling experiments with C\+FD can be complex as boundary conditions in experimental setups are constrained by geometrical, physical, and mechanical aspects, just to name a few. To accurately predict experiments, these boundary conditions need to be modeled precisely, which may require implementing a new model in Open\+F\+O\+AM that, for example, predicts the pressure drop and heat exchange in a pipe. The F\+MI standard addresses this issue by defining a container and interface to exchange multi-\/physical systems. The novel approach of F\+M\+U4\+F\+O\+AM enhances the modeling capabilities by combining the multi-\/physical domain models expressed by the F\+MI standard with Open\+F\+O\+AM. This approach simplifies not only the modeling of complex experiments but also allows users to verify simplifying assumptions in system level models. For example, a C\+FD simulation can easily replace a passenger cabin model in a \char`\"{}\+Heating Venting and Air Conditioning\char`\"{}-\/\+System to compare both modeling approaches. The same approach is also helpful in Guidance and Control to verify transfer function.
