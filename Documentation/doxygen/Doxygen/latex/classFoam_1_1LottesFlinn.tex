\subsection{Detailed Description}
Model in the spirit of Lottes-\/\+Flinn, i.\+e.\+: 

\label{_Description}%
 phi2 = 1/(alpha$^\wedge$exp)

with alpha being the normalized phase fraction (by the void fraction) of the multiplier\+Fluid and exp defaulting to 2 (which is the actual Lottes-\/\+Flinn model). The exp can be user-\/provided (it is know from e.\+g. T\+R\+A\+CE development that this exponent can be slightly smaller for bubbly flows, e.\+g. in the 1.\+7-\/1.\+8 range).
