\subsection*{Summary}

Accurate C\+FD simulations often require complex boundary conditions that may be depend on numerous other engineering disciplines. Currently, these boundary conditions would require the addition of new models to the open-\/source C\+FD framework Open\+F\+O\+AM which is error-\/prone and time-\/consuming. F\+MI standard offers a container (F\+M\+Us) to exchange multi-\/physics models that can be used to simulate these multi-\/physics. This library provides the possibility of coupled F\+M\+Us with Open\+F\+O\+AM or exporting Open\+F\+O\+AM as an F\+MU.
